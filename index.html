Anime Sketcher Privacy Policy

Developer: GLOZION · Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains how the Anime Sketcher Android app (package com.glozion.animesketch), developed by GLOZION ("we", "us"), handles your information. Anime Sketcher is designed to work on your device: we do not require an account, and we do not sell your data.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, contacts, or location. The app contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking software.

Camera

Camera access is used only to display a live tracing guide over your paper and to take a photo of your drawing when you choose to. Camera images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Video and audio are not recorded.

Photos and media

When you pick a photo, the app receives only the image you select. Drawings and references you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. If you choose "Save to Gallery", the image is saved to your device's photo library.

On-device trace conversion

Converting a colored image into line art runs entirely on your phone using a bundled model. Your images are not sent to any server for conversion.

Online template catalog

To show drawing templates, the app downloads category lists and template images from our catalog server hosted on Cloudflare. As with any internet request, the server and Cloudflare receive technical connection information such as your IP address, device/user-agent details, and the time of the request. We use this only to deliver the content and keep the service secure, and we do not use it to identify you. Cloudflare's handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, language preference, the template cache, and your saved drawings are stored locally on your device. Android's own backup settings may include some app data in your Google device backup; temporary trace caches are excluded.

Feedback and sharing

Nothing is sent until you choose to. If you send feedback, the app opens your email app addressed to us; we receive only what you include in that email and use it to reply and improve the app. When you share content, the app or service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual drawings in My Collection, remove favorites by tapping the heart, or clear all app data from Android Settings → Apps → Anime Sketcher → Storage. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Images already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. To ask us to delete a feedback email, contact us at the address below.

Children

Anime Sketcher does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the app does not collect personal information, no such data is stored by us.

Third-party software

The app includes the Informative Drawings line-art model by Caroline Chan (ONNX export by Kazuhito Takahashi) and ONNX Runtime by Microsoft. These run locally and do not send data to their authors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
