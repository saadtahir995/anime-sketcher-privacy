Anime Sketcher Privacy Policy

Developer: GLOZION · Effective date: October 28, 2026

This policy explains how the Anime Sketcher Android app (package com.glozion.animesketch), developed by GLOZION ("we", "us"), handles your information. Anime Sketcher is designed to work on your device: we do not require an account, and we do not sell your data.

Information we collect

You can use the app without creating an account. We do not upload your photos or drawings. Depending on your app version and whether advertising is enabled, Google AdMob and RevenueCat may process the technical and purchase information described below. Earlier versions without these integrations do not use those services.

Advertising and your choices

Versions with advertising use Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may collect device and advertising identifiers, IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device information, diagnostics, and interactions with ads to deliver, measure, secure and improve advertising. We do not request precise device location for ads. Where required, the app requests your consent before requesting advertisements. You can review or change your choices through "Ad privacy choices" in Settings when that option applies. Personalized, non-personalized or limited ads may be served depending on your choices and region. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from partner apps.

Remove ads purchases

Remove ads is an optional one-time, non-consumable Google Play purchase, not a subscription. Google Play handles payment and payment details; GLOZION does not receive your card information. RevenueCat processes an app user identifier, purchase identifiers and tokens, purchase history and status, and technical device information to validate purchases, provide ad removal and restore purchases. These records may be retained for entitlement management and legal obligations. No drawing or photo content is sent to RevenueCat. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy. Purchasing Remove ads stops banner and full-screen ad requests; purchase verification and template downloads still use network services.

Camera

Camera access is used only to display a live tracing guide over your paper and to take a photo of your drawing when you choose to. Camera images are processed on your device and are not uploaded to us. Video and audio are not recorded.

Photos and media

When you pick a photo, the app receives only the image you select. Drawings and references you save are stored in the app's private storage on your device. If you choose "Save to Gallery", the image is saved to your device's photo library.

On-device trace conversion

Converting a colored image into line art runs entirely on your phone using a bundled model. Your images are not sent to any server for conversion.

Online template catalog

To show drawing templates, the app downloads category lists and template images from our catalog server hosted on Cloudflare. As with any internet request, the server and Cloudflare receive technical connection information such as your IP address, device/user-agent details, and the time of the request. We use this only to deliver the content and keep the service secure, and we do not use it to identify you. Cloudflare's handling is described in the Cloudflare Privacy Policy.

Data stored on your device

Favorites, language preference, the template cache, and your saved drawings are stored locally on your device. Android's own backup settings may include some app data in your Google device backup; temporary trace caches are excluded.

Feedback and sharing

Nothing is sent until you choose to. If you send feedback, the app opens your email app addressed to us; we receive only what you include in that email and use it to reply and improve the app. When you share content, the app or service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

Deleting your data

You can delete individual drawings in My Collection, remove favorites by tapping the heart, or clear all app data from Android Settings → Apps → Anime Sketcher → Storage. Uninstalling the app removes its local data. Images already saved to your gallery stay there until you delete them. Uninstalling does not delete Google Play transactions or records held by providers. Contact info@glozion.com to request access to or deletion of information held by us or to ask about purchase-related records. Some records may need to be retained for legal, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes; provider policies describe their retention practices.

Children

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If you believe a child has provided information through the app or its services, contact info@glozion.com so we can investigate and address the request.

Third-party software

The app includes the Informative Drawings line-art model by Caroline Chan (ONNX export by Kazuhito Takahashi) and ONNX Runtime by Microsoft. These run locally and do not send data to their authors.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app changes. The effective date above shows the latest version.
